Privacy policy

Effective 26 September 2026

This policy covers the Veiled Pages iOS and Android apps and this website. The Veiled Pages team maintains the app and handles feedback. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@reasonswithin.com and mention Veiled Pages.

Your journal

Dream entries, reading logs, card meanings, symbols, tags, and feelings are stored on your device. You do not need a Veiled Pages account. We do not receive your journal through a Veiled Pages server, sell your personal information, or use your entries for advertising or AI training. The app does not include advertising or an analytics SDK.

Optional cloud sync

If you enable sync, the app sends journal snapshots to your private iCloud database on iOS or Google Drive app-data storage on Android, using your own provider account. Snapshots contain your journal records and information needed to restore them and preserve conflicting edits. Provider authorization is used to access that storage; the app does not ask for your Apple or Google password.

Ordinary sync relies on the provider’s account and storage security; it is not protected by a separate Veiled Pages end-to-end encryption key. iCloud and Google Drive are separate stores. Turn off sync in Settings to stop further syncing from that device. Disabling sync does not delete existing cloud copies or revoke provider permissions.

Backups and exports

Optional encrypted file backups use your recovery code. Keep the code separate from the backup; we cannot recover it for you. Ordinary JSON, Markdown, and PDF exports are unencrypted. You choose where to save or share files, and that destination’s privacy practices apply. iOS device backups may also contain app data; Android’s automatic app backup is disabled.

Permissions and device features

Voice capture uses microphone and speech-recognition permissions when you choose it. Transcription uses on-device recognition where available, without a cloud-recognition fallback; the app does not retain audio recordings. Device authentication is handled by the operating system, and Veiled Pages does not receive your biometric data. Optional reminders use local notifications. You can change permissions in your device settings.

Feedback and support

The feedback page embeds a Fillout form. When the form loads, your browser connects to Fillout; when you submit, Fillout processes your answers for review by the Veiled Pages team. We use feedback to answer questions, investigate problems, and improve the app. An email address is optional for a reply. Email feedback also reveals your sender address and is handled by email providers. Reviewed issues may be recorded in a private Trello board for follow-up.

The app’s feedback link includes only whether you came from iOS or Android. It does not attach journal entries, device identifiers, versions, logs, or screenshots. The website also accepts optional app and OS version parameters if a link supplies them. Use made-up examples instead of private journal entries, and never send a recovery code.

Website and beta services

GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. We do not add website analytics or advertising trackers. The privacy page itself does not embed the feedback form. Fillout may process technical information when its form loads, under its own privacy policy.

If you install a beta through TestFlight or Google Play, those services may collect testing, crash, device, and diagnostic information and make reports available to us under their settings and terms. This is separate from the journal stored by Veiled Pages.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local journal records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Settings includes a local-data deletion action. Delete cloud copies separately through the app’s cloud controls; disable sync on other devices first so they do not upload another copy. Deleting on one device does not erase exports, backups, or copies on other devices. Manage those copies and provider backups separately.

We keep identifiable feedback only as long as needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete feedback by emailing the contact above. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to your data-protection authority. We may need information to locate your submission and verify your request. We cannot directly retrieve or delete the journal stored on your device or in your personal cloud account.

Service providers

Providers may process information outside your country according to their policies. Their security, retention, and transfer practices are described here:

  • Apple privacy policy — iCloud and TestFlight.
  • Google privacy policy — Drive and Google Play.
  • GitHub privacy statement — website hosting.
  • Fillout privacy policy — feedback forms.
  • Atlassian privacy policy — Trello issue tracking.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes will also be called out in the app or beta release notes.
